Logo Proposals / v1

Three marks for NosoTrack.

All three are monochromatic single-weight glyphs sized to read at 16 px favicon through 140 px hero. Each includes a single red accent that reuses the product's alert token — maintaining the narrative that the brand chrome itself signals outbreak detection. No gradients, no drop-shadows, no decorative flourish. Pick one and I'll wire it into the nav, footer, favicon, and OG card.
