Outbreak data are fragmented. The response cannot be.

Outbreak investigation remains largely manual.Teams piece together contact tracing, clinical records and microbiology results through ad hoc analyses. The process is slow, labour-intensive, inconsistent and difficult to audit.Nosotrack fuses these data into a unified Bayesian inference engine powered by the open-source, peer-reviewed outbreaker2 model12 to identify transmission chains in near real time, revealing the drivers of disease transmission and enabling targeted control strategies.

Nosotrack

Every outbreak leaves a trail. Nosotrack finds it.

Nosotrack reconstructs the transmission tree.Identifying the sources of infection and how disease spreads is critical to devising targeted control strategies.Nosotrack provides a consistent visual interface to the outbreaker2 engine identifying the most likely index case, imported cases, superspreaders, undetected cases, transmission patterns and individuals at risk. Additional developments will help identify potential sources of contamination, such as medical equipment.

Pathogen agnostic, deployable anywhere.

Nosotrack provides the operating system for infectious disease response.We aim to deliver a complete outbreak intelligence service, combining analytics, contact-tracing hardware, rapid diagnostics, sequencing and field epidemiology. Our technology strives to support outbreak responses across all scales, from Candida auris in hospitals and swine flu on farms to WHO-coordinated responses to global health emergencies such as Ebola.
