NOSOTRACK Nosocomial Outbreaks
The Problem

Healthcare-associated infections are deadly, costly, and poorly managed.

Healthcare-associated infections affect 1 in 10 patients worldwide. Current control measures rely on broad testing and isolation. Effective intervention requires identifying the specific drivers of an outbreak: patient zero, superspreaders, transmission routes, and undetected or imported cases.

NOSOTRACK The Impact
The Impact

Who benefits, and why it matters.

Over 400 US Academic Medical Centers already implement routine genomic surveillance. High-acuity, high-cost populations such as transplant, ICU, and immunocompromised patients carry the steepest federal penalty exposure for HAIs, with dedicated prevention teams already in place.

Reduced morbidity Fewer healthcare-associated infections through earlier outbreak detection.
Reduced mortality Earlier containment of high-risk transmission chains saves lives.
Economic savings Lower bed-day costs, fewer ward closures, reduced federal HAI penalties.
Improved operations Prevention teams shift from reactive protocols to targeted, evidence-based control.
Pandemic preparedness Real-time forensics infrastructure ready for the next pathogen.
Scalable reach Applicable beyond hospitals to nursing homes and long-term care facilities.
