# Hospital furniture artwork

Selected sprites from **Hospital Top-Down Pixel Pack** by **Soppycraft**.
Source: https://soppycraft.itch.io/top-down-hospital
Downloaded 28 September 2026. Used as furniture within the Nosotrack integration animation.
The renderer adapts the colours to Nosotrack's existing neutral palette. Architecture,
characters, movement, clinical signals and transmission overlays are original code.
